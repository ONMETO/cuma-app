Frequently Asked Questions

What is Bagel Muse?

Bagel Muse is an iOS music app combining BPM-based workout pacing with AI-generated mood playlists and visual album art stories.

How does Bagel Muse sync music to my pace?

It pairs the beat-per-minute (BPM) tempo of songs with your movement cadence to keep you in sync during running, gym sessions, and daily activities.
